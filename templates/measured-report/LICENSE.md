# Design and cover provenance

The measured.one layout and “Signal field” cover are original work created for Goldberg Consulting LLC. The artwork consists entirely of vector curves in measured-report.sty; it contains no stock photograph, third-party logo, or externally sourced image. It is decorative, not client data.
